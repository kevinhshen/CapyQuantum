CAPTAIN CAPY’S POCKET MANUAL

This is a game about engineering under pressure.

01 / Fly

Thrust with WASD or arrows. Momentum matters; Shift brakes and Space dashes. Collect amber scrap. Avoid rocks, the planet, and the scanner beams. Stay alive until the shift ends.

02 / Engineer

Each cargo pulse runs your encoder through a real quantum-state simulation. Place gates, choose a detection basis, and map syndromes to recovery actions. Better fidelity means more cargo and credits.

03 / Adapt

The Bit Belt injects X errors; the Phase Garden injects Z errors. A bit shelter can’t protect both. The last route announces changing weather: pause with E and switch your design before the next pulse.

04 / Progress

Clear contracts to open routes. Buy hull, engines, a recycler fleet, or a salvage magnet. Failure still pays for the cargo you delivered. Retry and improve the design.

How to build from scratch

Choose BARE WIRE, then CLEAR ALL. Place CX: q0→q1 in column 1, q0→q2 in column 2. Use Z detection. The checks are q0⊕q1 and q0⊕q2; set 00→none, 01→q2, 10→q1, 11→q0. That is a bit shelter. For a phase sail, add H on all three qubits after those CX gates, and switch detection to X.

The boundary of the simulation

Errors are selected Pauli operations; encoding, detection, and recovery gates are ideal. This is not realistic hardware reliability, arbitrary error correction, or a quantum speed advantage. Each pulse is a fresh state and one correction round. The six-state score avoids judging only one convenient input. Fidelity is an internal simulator diagnostic.

Credits, spaceships, and atmospheric effects are game fiction. There is no physical quantum-computer job in the browser. Qiskit export and local verification reproduce your actual circuit. Qollab upload and publication require a separate platform test.
